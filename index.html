Plan it before you build it.

Rearwall AI draws up your design and planning drawings, runs your structural calculations and prices every part of the build, for your house extension.

What Rearwall does

  1. Draws up your design and planning drawings

    Existing and proposed floor plans, elevations and a site plan.

  2. Runs your structural calculations

    Beams, lintels and foundations for the new work.

  3. Prices every part of the build

    A cost plan, line by line, from groundworks to finishes.

How it works

  1. Tell Rearwall AI about your house and what you would like to build.
  2. Rearwall AI prepares your drawings, calculations and costs.
  3. You get one build pack. See every drawing and figure before you commit.

Questions

Which extensions can I plan?
  • Rear extensions
  • Side return extensions
  • Wraparound extensions
  • Single-storey and two-storey work
Can I build straight from the pack?

No. Rearwall's drawings and calculations need checking by a qualified professional before use. Rearwall is not an architect's practice, a structural engineer or a surveyor, and its cost plan is an estimate, not a builder's quote.

Will this get me planning permission?

Rearwall cannot promise that. The decision is your council's, and the rules differ across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

What happens to my details?

This page has no forms, so nothing you could type or upload is stored. Rearwall is currently a research prototype; see About this study.
